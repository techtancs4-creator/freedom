NOTICE.

A plain-language summary of what this website stores. It is not legal advice.

What we store

  • Your username, email address and a securely hashed password (we can't read your password).
  • Minecraft usernames you submit for whitelist requests and rank orders, plus payment transaction hashes you submit.
  • Support tickets and the replies you receive.
  • When your account was created and last logged in.

Why

Only to run the community: let you log in, review whitelist requests, verify rank payments and answer support. We don't sell your data and the site has no advertising or analytics trackers.

Who can see it

Server staff with admin access can see account details and requests so they can do their job. Other players can't see your email.

Cookies and third parties

The site uses a session cookie to keep you logged in and protect forms. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the live server status may be checked through a public status service if the server can't be queried directly.

Deleting your data
